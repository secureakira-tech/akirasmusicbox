User Journey Flows

Five journeys from the PRD, mapped as interaction flows. Step 10 of bmad-create-ux-design.

JOURNEY 01 The Discovery Reader Marcus — cold entry, highest stakes
Arrives via external link (Slack, social, forward). Skeptical. Decides within 3 seconds. The read-listen loop is the conversion event; Bandcamp click and newsletter subscribe are the exits.

JOURNEY 02 The Subscriber Priya — weekly ritual, established trust
Arrives via Buttondown email. Knows what the site is. Friction is lower everywhere. Archive browsability is important — she goes back to posts she skipped.

JOURNEY 03 The Hi-Fi Curious Reader Daniel — sidebar scanner, archive browser
Arrives via audiophile referral. Reading is secondary — he's looking for the sidebar. Archive browsability by sidebar presence is structurally load-bearing for this journey.
flowchart TD A([External link\nr/audiophile / referral]) --> B[Post page loads] B --> C[Reading prose] C --> D[Hi-fi sidebar\ncaught in scan\ngreen zone, right gutter] D --> E[Read sidebar\nProduction Note / Listener Gloss] E --> F[Listen with new frame\nheadphones vs. speakers] F --> G{Explore more?} G -->|No| H([Finish post or close]) G -->|Yes| I[Archive page] I --> J[Filter: hi-fi sidebar posts] J --> K[Post list\nhi-fi-tagged posts] K --> L[Select post] L --> M[Post page:\nsidebar visible\nin right gutter] M --> E
JOURNEY 04 The Author — Nominal Publish Week Akira — Tuesday, buffer at 2, no surprises
Fast, local, self-contained. Push → deploy → queue. All error recovery loops back to local preview before push.

JOURNEY 05 The Author — Opera Week Akira — 2 hours, buffer at 1, no sidebar required
Identical path to Journey 4 but shorter: no sidebar, one embed, 400 words minimum. Short-form post path must feel identical in quality to standard — same design, less content.

Journey Patterns
Entry → Trust signal → Play
The spine of every reader journey. The page has one job in the first 3 seconds: prove it's real. Typography and silence do this before a word is read.
Read-listen loop
Not a step — a mode. The design can't force it, only allow it. CLS = 0 and embed placement in prose are the enabling conditions. Once entered, the loop is self-sustaining.
Support action as natural conclusion
Bandcamp / follow links appear after the read-listen loop has done its work. The journey earns the click — the design does not manufacture urgency before the reader is ready.
Sidebar as parallel track
Daniel's journey is structurally different from Marcus's — he's scanning, not reading linearly. The sidebar must be visually detectable from the prose column without interrupting linear readers who aren't looking for it.
Author loop is self-contained
The authoring journey has no dependency on reader behavior. Push → deploy → queue is three steps with one error-recovery path. Everything else is local preview.
